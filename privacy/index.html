Privacy Policy

Prxcess Apps respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This general privacy policy informs you about how we look after your personal data when you visit our website or use our applications, and tells you about your privacy rights and how the law protects you.

We may collect, use, store and transfer different kinds of personal data about you depending on the application you use. Please review the specific app sections below for detailed information on what data each application collects and how it is used.

App-Specific Policies

Kindo

Active
expand_more

1. Developer Information

The Kindo app is developed and maintained by Prxcess Apps.

2. Data Collection & Usage

We collect the following information to provide and improve our service:

  • Personal Information: We collect your email address and generate unique User IDs for account creation and management, powered by Firebase Auth. If provided during onboarding, we may also store your name.
  • Financial Information: We collect your purchase history to track subscription status, active entitlements, and past renewals via RevenueCat. We do not collect or process credit card information directly; this is securely handled by the App Store or Google Play.
  • App Activity & User-Generated Content: We store content you create within the app to provide core functionality, such as syncing chores, tasks, and notes, using Firebase Cloud Firestore.
  • Device or Other IDs: We collect device installation IDs automatically via Firebase and RevenueCat for fraud prevention, analytics, and ensuring your purchases sync correctly across your devices.

3. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services to ensure the app functions correctly, including processing payments:

  • Google Play: Used for app distribution and in-app purchases.
  • RevenueCat: Used to manage and process subscriptions and payments.

4. Data Deletion

You have the right to request the deletion of your Kindo account and associated data. You can delete your account instantly using the form below:

Instant Account Deletion

Enter your Kindo credentials to instantly and permanently delete your account and all associated data.

OR

Alternatively, you can delete your account from within the Kindo app (Profile > Settings > Delete Account) or email us at support@prxcess.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request".

SnapMo

Release Ready
expand_more

1. Developer Information

The SnapMo app is developed and maintained by Prxcess Apps. Contact us at support@prxcess.com.

2. Data Collection, Usage, and Sharing

We collect and use data strictly to provide the SnapMo stop-motion animation studio experience. We do not collect, store, sell, or share personal data or children's information with third parties.

  • Camera Access: We request camera permissions solely to display the real-time viewfinder and capture animation frames. All frame captures and onion-skin overlays are processed 100% on-device and stored in local internal app storage. Image data is never transmitted over the internet or sent to external servers.
  • Storage & Photos / Media Access: We request photo library write access solely when you explicitly tap to export a finished movie, saving the MP4 video directly to your device's camera roll. We never read, scan, or access any other files in your photo library.
  • Audio & Sound: SnapMo features a procedural audio engine and sound effect soundboard that synthesizes sound locally in real-time via the Web Audio API. We do not record voice, microphone audio, or speech biometrics.
  • Personal & Device Identifiers: SnapMo does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, IP addresses, or Google Advertising IDs (AAID).
  • No Account Required: SnapMo requires no login, passwords, sign-ups, or user accounts.

3. Google Play Families Policy & Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

SnapMo is engineered specifically for children and families. We adhere strictly to the Google Play Families Policy, the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), and GDPR-K:

  • Zero Advertising: SnapMo contains no commercial advertisements, behavioral tracking, ad networks, or banner promotions.
  • No In-App Purchases: There are no microtransactions, hidden paywalls, or subscription models.
  • No External Links: The app does not expose children to external web links, social media feeds, or public forums.
  • Child Safety: We do not knowingly solicit or collect personally identifiable information from children of any age.

4. Third-Party Services

To guarantee complete privacy and child safety, SnapMo does not integrate any third-party tracking, analytics, or advertising SDKs (e.g. no Google Analytics, no Firebase Analytics, no AdMob, no Meta Pixel). All video encoding, image compression, and procedural audio synthesis execute 100% locally on your device hardware with zero cloud dependencies.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Because SnapMo operates entirely on-device, we retain zero user data on any cloud servers or external databases. You retain complete control over your creative work:

  • In-App Deletion: You can delete individual frames or entire movie projects at any time within the app (tap the frame's Delete button or manage projects in the Projects menu).
  • Complete Data Removal: To permanently delete all SnapMo data, cache, and projects from your device, simply clear the app's storage in your device settings (Settings > Apps > SnapMo > Storage > Clear Data) or uninstall the app. All local databases and cached frames will be immediately and irreversibly purged from your hardware.

For any privacy-related questions or compliance inquiries, please contact us at support@prxcess.com.

Musicool

Testing
expand_more

1. Developer Information

The Musicool app is developed and maintained by Prxcess Apps. Contact us at support@prxcess.com.

2. Data Collection, Usage, and Sharing

We collect and use your data strictly to provide and improve the Musicool service. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

  • Profile & Progress Data: Nicknames, chosen avatars, daily practice streaks, calibration tiers, and sticker achievements are stored locally on your device (localStorage) to personalize daily training sessions. This data is never sent to external servers.
  • Audio & Sound Processing: The app uses the Web Audio API strictly for on-device real-time synthesizer tone playback and ear training. No microphone audio is recorded, stored, or transmitted.
  • Children’s Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy): Musicool is designed for young learners. We do not collect names, emails, phone numbers, location data, or persistent advertising identifiers from children.
  • Location Data: We do not request, access, or track device location data.
  • Camera Access: We do not request or access camera permissions.

3. Google Play Families & User Data Compliance

Musicool adheres to the Google Play Families Policy, COPPA, and the Google API Services User Data Policy:

  • Zero Behavioral Ads & Tracking: The app contains no third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral trackers, or user profiling.
  • Safe Learning Sandbox: All games, auditory exercises, and visual tools run client-side in a secure, age-appropriate environment.

4. Third-Party Services

Musicool operates client-side with minimal dependencies:

  • No Analytics SDKs: We do not embed Google Analytics, tracking cookies, or tracking pixels.
  • Local Sound Synthesis: Audio tones are generated in real-time via the browser's native Web Audio API without third-party audio service dependencies.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all training records and profiles are stored locally on your device, you have complete control over your data at all times. Here is how to delete your Musicool data:

  1. Open the Musicool app.
  2. Go to Profile / Settings in the top navigation bar.
  3. Tap "Delete Profile" or "Clear All Data" and confirm.

Alternatively, clearing your browser storage/cache or uninstalling the app permanently purges all profile records from your device immediately.

Parents and users may also contact us at support@prxcess.com with the subject "Child Data / Privacy Request" with any questions or deletion requests.

BrewBalance

Testing
expand_more

1. Developer Information

The BrewBalance app is developed and maintained by Prxcess Apps. Contact us at support@prxcess.com.

2. Data Collection, Usage, and Sharing

We collect and use your data strictly to provide and improve the BrewBalance service. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

  • Authentication Data: We collect your email address via Firebase Auth solely to create and manage your account.
  • Health & Intake Data: We store your daily caffeine intake and biometric sleep scores in Firestore to provide personalized insights and analytics within the app.
  • Location Data: We request foreground location access strictly to locate nearby coffee shops. This data is processed temporarily and is never stored on our servers.
  • Camera Access: We request camera permissions solely to scan barcodes locally on your device. Image data is processed on-device and never transmitted or saved.

3. Google API Services Compliance

BrewBalance's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We ensure that data retrieved via Google Maps or Google Authentication is used exclusively to provide the core app functionality and is never used for serving ads.

4. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services to ensure the app functions correctly:

  • Google Maps Platform: Used for location-based coffee shop discovery.
  • Open Food Facts API: Used to retrieve nutritional data from scanned barcodes.
  • Fitbit API: Used to integrate wearable health and sleep data via Fitbit's open API.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your data only for as long as your account is active. You have the right to request the complete deletion of your BrewBalance account and all associated data at any time. Here is how to delete your BrewBalance account:

  1. Open the BrewBalance app and log in.
  2. Go to your Profile / Settings.
  3. Tap on "Delete Account" and confirm.

Alternatively, you can email us at support@prxcess.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request" from the email address associated with your account, and all user data (including health logs and authentication records) will be permanently deleted from our databases within 30 days.
